Knicks Parade

Set my alarm for 6:30. The Knicks were getting their parade and I wasn't going to miss it. I got to Fifth Avenue early, found a position with a clean line of sight to the Canyon of Heroes, and spent the next few hours shooting the motorcade as it pushed through the city. Confetti falling, crowds pressed against the barricades, players waving from the floats — the kind of New York moment that doesn't come around often. No client, no brief. Just me, a camera, and a city that had been waiting a long time for this.
