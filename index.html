About

Hello, I'm Colin Chow, a creative director and producer based in New York and the Bay Area.

I am currently a student at NYU Tisch studying Film & Television with a minor in Business of Entertainment, Media, and Technology. I am passionate about driving growth through creative marketing and storytelling and hope to build a career at the intersection of entertainment, business, and creative production.

I picked up a camera during the pandemic and never really put it down. What started as curiosity turned into a fascination with how moving images can make people feel something. Since then, I've produced branded content for companies like TikTok, a16z, AWS, and Notion. I also run my own production company, where I take on projects that push my creative practice further, offering services in cinematography, editing, and color.
